Customer Policies & Terms of Service

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Fortine's Repair is a small, family-owned electronics repair business serving the Yuba-Sutter area, including Live Oak and Plumas Lake.

We provide mail-in repair services for game consoles, televisions, laptops, desktop computers, and phones.

Please read these policies before submitting a device for repair.

1. Repair Estimates and Customer Approval

  • Devices may require inspection before an accurate repair estimate can be provided.
  • Any diagnostic fee will be disclosed to the customer and approved before it is charged.
  • Customers will receive an estimate before repair work begins.
  • No additional work or charges beyond the approved estimate will be made without customer authorization.
  • If a device cannot be repaired, the customer will be informed of available options and any previously approved charges.

2. Mail-In Shipping Policy

Fortine's Repair currently operates on a mail-in basis.

  • Customers are responsible for properly packaging devices to prevent shipping damage.
  • Customers are responsible for shipping costs to Fortine's Repair.
  • Customers must provide accurate return shipping information.
  • Only accessories necessary for diagnosis or repair should be included.
  • Customers should remove SIM cards, memory cards, cases, and personal accessories unless needed for the repair.
  • Return shipping costs will be communicated before the repaired device is shipped back.

3. Payment and Refund Policy

  • Any diagnostic fee, parts cost, deposit, or other charge will be disclosed before it is charged.
  • Customers must approve repair estimates before additional repair work begins.
  • Payment arrangements will be communicated before the device is returned.
  • Refund requests will be reviewed based on work performed, parts ordered, and applicable consumer protection laws.
  • Nothing in this policy removes rights customers have under applicable law.

4. Privacy and Customer Data

Customers are strongly encouraged to back up their devices before sending them for repair.

Fortine's Repair cannot guarantee that stored data, files, photographs, applications, or settings will remain intact during diagnosis or repair.

  • Back up important information before shipping.
  • Remove personal or sensitive information when practical.
  • Remove SIM cards and memory cards unless needed for testing.
  • Avoid sharing account passwords unless necessary for an agreed repair or test.

Customer contact information will be used to communicate about repair requests, estimates, payments, shipping, and customer service.

Fortine's Repair will take reasonable steps to protect customer information.

5. Liability and Device Condition

Fortine's Repair will use reasonable care when receiving, inspecting, repairing, and handling customer devices.

Electronics may have pre-existing defects, hidden damage, worn components, or other conditions that affect repair results.

Fortine's Repair cannot guarantee:

  • That every device can be repaired.
  • That a repair will restore every original manufacturer feature or specification.
  • That customer data will remain intact.
  • That a device will retain its original water resistance after being opened or repaired.

Fortine's Repair is not responsible for unrelated pre-existing failures or damage caused by events outside its reasonable control.

Nothing in this policy excludes responsibility for damage caused by our negligence, intentional misconduct, or any responsibility that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

6. Customer Responsibilities and Terms of Service

  • Customers must own the device or have permission from its owner to authorize the repair.
  • Customers must provide accurate contact and shipping information.
  • Customers must review and approve applicable estimates and charges.
  • Repair results can vary depending on the device's condition and availability of replacement parts.

Fortine's Repair may decline a repair request when a device is unsafe to handle, parts are unavailable, or the requested repair cannot reasonably be performed.

Additional work requires customer approval.

7. Independent Repair Business

Fortine's Repair is an independent electronics repair business. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or officially authorized by Sony, Microsoft, Apple, Samsung, or other electronics manufacturers unless expressly stated otherwise.

Manufacturer names and trademarks are used only to identify compatible devices and services.
